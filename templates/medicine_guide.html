Safer medicine-use checklist

  • Read the package leaflet and follow the label or prescriber's instructions; do not guess a dose.
  • Check the active ingredient so you do not accidentally take two products with the same ingredient.
  • Tell the pharmacist about all prescription medicines, over-the-counter products, vitamins, supplements, allergies and health conditions.
  • For babies and children, do not estimate an adult dose or use an adult product without qualified advice.
  • Older adults, pregnant or breastfeeding people, and people with kidney or liver problems should get medicine-specific advice.
  • Do not start, stop, combine or change prescribed medicines based only on this website.
  • If a person has severe breathing difficulty, collapse, seizures, or a suspected serious overdose, seek emergency medical help immediately.

What this tool can and cannot tell you

Can help with

  • Checking whether a name appears in the supplied reference list.
  • Screening medicine pairs for records in the supplied interaction dataset.
  • Creating prompts to review age, allergies, conditions and kidney-function context.

Cannot safely determine yet

  • What medicine a person should take for a symptom.
  • A dose for a child or adult, or a dose adjustment for kidney function.
  • Whether a medicine is safe for a specific allergy, pregnancy, or health condition.
  • That a medicine list is safe just because no alert appears.
